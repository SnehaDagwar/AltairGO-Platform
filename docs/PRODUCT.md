# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Discerning, modern, and tech-savvy travelers planning mid-to-high-end leisure trips. They value personalized curation, hate planning fatigue, and want a smooth transition from inspiration to execution (booking, budgeting, and live daily logistics).

## Product Purpose
AltairGO Platform provides traveler-facing travel intelligence, bridging AI-powered itinerary generation with robust real-time booking management, expense tracking, and offline-ready daily briefings. Success is reducing travel stress, offering high agency over AI decisions, and presenting a unified view of the entire trip lifecycle.

## Brand Personality
- **Refined**: Elegant, premium feel that respects the traveler's intelligence and taste.
- **Intelligent**: Calmly analytical, using smart data visualization and semantic search to organize options.
- **Scenic**: Evoking the open world, adventure, and high-end discovery through cinematic scale and airy layouts.
- **Voice & Tone**: Empathetic, expert, and precise. Never patronizing, pushy, or corporate.

## Anti-references
- **Urgency-driven booking engines** (e.g., Expedia or Booking.com) with flashing red text, popups, and dark patterns that induce anxiety.
- **Generic corporate SaaS interfaces** using flat gray cards, standard blue primary buttons, and stock illustrations.
- **Cluttered travel portals** that compromise usability by trying to display too much data at once.

## Design Principles
- **Editorial Harmony**: The interface should feel as elegant and readable as a premium travel journal, prioritizing beautiful typographic scale and generous negative space over dense bento-box layouts.
- **User-in-the-Loop Agency**: Treat AI recommendations as fluid drafts. The traveler remains the editor, with intuitive actions to swap, override, edit, or reject any AI suggestion.
- **Contextual Clarity**: Information morphs logically based on the phase of the trip—from planning (wizard flow) to preparation (booking checklists) to execution (mobile-first daily briefings).
- **Subtle Motion & Depth**: Use smooth, hardware-accelerated micro-animations (e.g., staggered tab entrances, fluid slide-overs) to establish hierarchy, avoiding jarring jumps or unnecessary visual noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Fully WCAG 2.1 AA compliant.
- Highly visible focus indicators and explicit aria labels for complex tab and interactive drag-and-drop systems.
- High-contrast, custom OKLCH colors ensuring readable text ratios under bright outdoor sunlight or low-light cabin environments.
- Responsive to user preference for reduced motion through pure-CSS media query fallbacks.
